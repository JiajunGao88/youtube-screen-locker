# YouTube Screen Locker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

YouTube Screen Locker (published by BlueWave) does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

- **What is stored:** only your lock settings (for example, which shortcuts to block and whether deep lock is on). They are saved locally in your browser with `chrome.storage.local` and never leave your device.
- **Network:** the extension makes no network requests and uses no analytics, tracking, or remote code.
- **Permissions:** `activeTab`, `storage`, and `scripting` are used only to show the lock overlay and block clicks, keyboard shortcuts, and media keys on youtube.com pages.
- **Third parties:** no data is shared with anyone.
